# HEALTHCARE-ANALYSIS-
This project demonstrates the ability to perform end-to-end data analysis using Power BI — from raw data cleaning to insight generation. The dashboard provides actionable insights that can help hospital management optimize resources, control costs, and improve patient outcomes through data-driven decisions.

##  Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing hospital patient data to uncover insights related to patient admissions, treatment costs, department utilization, doctor workload, and care outcomes. Using Power BI, I performed end-to-end data analysis including data cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard development to support data-driven healthcare decision-making.
The goal of this project is to demonstrate practical analytics skills and the ability to translate raw healthcare data into meaningful business insights.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze patient admission trends over time
- Monitor hospital efficiency using cost and length of stay metrics
- Evaluate department and doctor workload distribution
- Track patient outcomes and quality indicators
- Perform Month-over-Month (MoM) performance analysis

## Data Preparation & Transformation
The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query:
- Removed duplicates and handled missing values
- Standardized column data types
- Created calculated columns such as:
- Length of Stay
- Age Group
- Split data into Fact and Dimension tables
- Built a Star Schema data model
- Created and marked a Date Table to enable time intelligence analysis
## The data model follows best practices:
- Fact Table: Patient Visits
- Dimension Tables: Date, Department, Doctor, Patient
- One-to-many relationships
- Optimized for DAX calculations and performance

##  Dashboard Features
The Power BI dashboard includes:
KPI cards for high-level performance monitoring
Line charts for monthly patient and cost trends
Bar charts for department and doctor workload analysis
Slicers for interactive filtering (Date, Department, Doctor)
MoM analysis using DAX time intelligence
This dashboard provides actionable insights into hospital performance, highlighting trends in patient volume, cost efficiency, department utilization, and care outcomes to support data-driven healthcare management.
1.	Monthly analysis Trend 
Observation:
Patient admissions show noticeable fluctuations across months, with peaks in certain periods.
Evidence:
Monthly admissions line chart.
Impact:
Periods of high admissions can strain hospital resources and staff.
Recommendation:
Plan staffing and inventory proactively during peak months.
2. Treatment Cost Behavior
Observation:
Treatment costs rise in months with higher patient admissions but not always proportionally.
Evidence:
Treatment cost trend vs patient trend.
Impact:
This indicates variability in treatment intensity and cost per patient.
Recommendation:
Review cost drivers in high-spend months to improve efficiency.
3. Department Utilization
Observation:
Some departments handle significantly more patients than others.
Evidence:
Patients by department bar chart.
Impact:
Overutilized departments risk burnout and reduced quality of care.
Recommendation:
Reallocate resources or hire additional staff for high-demand departments.
4.  Doctor Workload Imbalance
Observation:
Patient distribution across doctors is uneven.
Evidence:
Patients by doctor chart.
Impact:
Uneven workload may affect service quality and doctor performance.
Recommendation:
Implement more balanced patient assignment strategies.
5.  Mortality Rate Insight
Observation:
Mortality rate remains relatively low overall.
Evidence:
Mortality rate KPI.
Impact:
Indicates generally effective patient care, but continuous monitoring is necessary.
Recommendation:
Investigate departments or periods with higher mortality for quality improvement.
6. Patient Demographics
Observation:
Adult and senior patients form the majority of hospital admissions.
Evidence:
Age group and gender visuals.
Impact:
Higher prevalence of chronic conditions may increase treatment costs.
Recommendation:
Strengthen preventive and chronic care programs.
## Key Insights
Patient admissions fluctuate monthly, indicating seasonal demand patterns
Some departments consistently handle higher patient volumes, suggesting potential resource strain
Treatment costs do not always increase proportionally with patient volume, indicating variability in care intensity
Doctor workload distribution is uneven, highlighting the need for better patient allocation
Mortality rate remains relatively low but requires continuous monitoring to maintain quality of care
